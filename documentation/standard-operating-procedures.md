# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

## Purpose

Standard Operating Procedures ensure that every support agent delivers a consistent customer experience.

---

# SOP 1 – New Ticket

- Review ticket details.
- Verify customer information.
- Assign category and priority.
- Acknowledge receipt.

---

# SOP 2 – Ticket Resolution

- Investigate issue.
- Provide solution.
- Confirm customer satisfaction.
- Close ticket.

---

# SOP 3 – Escalation

- Escalate when SLA risk exists.
- Document actions taken.
- Notify supervisor.

---

# Benefits

- Consistent service
- Improved quality
- Reduced errors
- Better customer experience
