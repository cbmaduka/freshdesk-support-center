# Customer Personas

## Purpose

This document defines the primary customer groups that will interact with the Freshdesk Support Center. Understanding customer needs helps ensure support processes, SLAs, automations, and knowledge base articles are designed around real user expectations.

---

# Persona 1 – New Customer

## Profile

A first-time customer seeking information before making a purchase.

### Typical Needs

- Product information
- Pricing enquiries
- Delivery information
- Business hours
- General enquiries

### Preferred Support Channels

- Email
- Website Contact Form
- Live Chat

### Success Measure

Receives clear, accurate information quickly enough to make a purchasing decision.

---

# Persona 2 – Returning Customer

## Profile

An existing customer requiring assistance after a previous purchase.

### Typical Needs

- Order status
- Product questions
- Returns or exchanges
- Billing enquiries
- Account updates

### Preferred Support Channels

- Email
- Customer Portal

### Success Measure

Issues are resolved efficiently with minimal effort from the customer.

---

# Persona 3 – VIP Customer

## Profile

A high-value customer requiring priority support.

### Typical Needs

- Priority assistance
- Faster response times
- Personalised communication
- Escalated issue handling

### Preferred Support Channels

- Dedicated Email
- Phone Support

### Success Measure

Receives priority service that reflects the value of the relationship.

---

# Key Takeaways

The support centre should be designed to provide:

- Fast responses
- Clear communication
- Consistent service
- Appropriate ticket prioritisation
- Excellent customer experience
