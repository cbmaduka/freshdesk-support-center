# Customer Personas

## Purpose

This document identifies the primary customer groups that will interact with the Freshdesk Support Center. Understanding these personas ensures that support workflows, SLAs, automations, and knowledge base content are designed around real customer needs.

---

# Persona 1 – New Customer

## Description

A prospective customer seeking information before making a purchase.

### Typical Enquiries

- Product information
- Pricing
- Business hours
- Delivery options
- Promotions

### Preferred Channels

- Email
- Website Contact Form
- Live Chat

---

# Persona 2 – Existing Customer

## Description

A returning customer requiring assistance after purchasing.

### Typical Enquiries

- Order status
- Returns and exchanges
- Billing questions
- Product support
- Account updates

### Preferred Channels

- Email
- Customer Portal

---

# Persona 3 – VIP Customer

## Description

A high-value customer requiring priority service.

### Typical Enquiries

- Priority support
- Escalated complaints
- Account management
- Special requests

### Preferred Channels

- Dedicated Email
- Phone Support

---

# Design Considerations

The support centre should provide:

- Fast responses
- Consistent communication
- Clear ticket ownership
- Appropriate prioritisation
- Excellent customer experience
