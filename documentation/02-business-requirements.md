# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project

Freshdesk Support Center Implementation

---

## Business Background

The business currently receives customer enquiries through multiple channels including email, phone calls, social media, and website contact forms. Without a centralized support system, customer requests are difficult to track, response times are inconsistent, and management has limited visibility into support performance.

The objective of this project is to implement a structured Freshdesk support centre that improves customer experience, standardises support processes, and provides actionable reporting.

---

## Business Objectives

- Centralise customer support requests
- Improve first response and resolution times
- Standardise ticket handling procedures
- Reduce manual work using automation
- Build a searchable customer knowledge base
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Generate meaningful support reports

---

## Project Scope

### In Scope

- Freshdesk setup
- Ticket categories
- Ticket priorities
- SLA configuration
- Automations
- Email notifications
- Knowledge Base
- Reporting dashboards

### Out of Scope

- Billing integrations
- Third-party API development
- Custom application development
