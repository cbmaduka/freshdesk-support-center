# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Policy

## Purpose

This document defines expected response and resolution times for customer enquiries.

---

# Priority Levels

| Priority | First Response | Resolution Target |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Low | 8 Hours | 3 Business Days |
| Medium | 4 Hours | 2 Business Days |
| High | 2 Hours | 1 Business Day |
| Urgent | 1 Hour | 4 Hours |

---

# Business Hours

Monday – Friday

9:00 AM – 5:00 PM

---

# Escalation

Tickets approaching SLA breach should automatically notify supervisors and be reassigned where necessary.

---

# Objectives

- Improve customer expectations
- Reduce missed deadlines
- Ensure consistent service delivery
