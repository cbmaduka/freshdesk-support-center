# Escalation Matrix

## Purpose

The escalation matrix defines how unresolved or high-priority tickets move through the support organisation.

---

| Level | Owner | Example |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Level 1 | Customer Support Agent | General enquiries |
| Level 2 | Senior Support Agent | Technical issues |
| Level 3 | Support Manager | Customer complaints |
| Level 4 | Business Owner | Critical business issues |

---

# Escalation Triggers

- SLA breach
- VIP customer
- Multiple customer contacts
- High business impact
- Security concerns

---

# Goals

- Faster issue resolution
- Clear ownership
- Improved accountability
