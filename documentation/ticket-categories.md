# Ticket Categories

## Purpose

Ticket categories provide a consistent way to classify customer enquiries, improve reporting, and support workflow automation.

---

# Categories

## General Enquiry

Questions about products, services or business information.

---

## Sales

Pre-sales enquiries, quotations and product recommendations.

---

## Orders

Order status, delivery tracking and purchase enquiries.

---

## Billing

Invoices, payments, refunds and account charges.

---

## Technical Support

Product issues, troubleshooting and technical assistance.

---

## Complaints

Service complaints requiring investigation or escalation.

---

## Feedback

Customer compliments, suggestions and recommendations.

---

# Benefits

Standard ticket categories help:

- Improve reporting
- Enable automation
- Reduce response times
- Simplify routing
- Improve customer experience
