# SLA Template

## Service Level Agreement

### First Response Time

| Priority | Response Time |
|----------|---------------|
| Low | 24 Hours |
| Medium | 8 Hours |
| High | 4 Hours |
| Critical | 1 Hour |

---

### Resolution Targets

| Priority | Resolution Time |
|----------|-----------------|
| Low | 3 Business Days |
| Medium | 2 Business Days |
| High | 8 Hours |
| Critical | 4 Hours |

---

## Escalation

Tickets approaching an SLA breach should automatically be escalated to the appropriate support level.
