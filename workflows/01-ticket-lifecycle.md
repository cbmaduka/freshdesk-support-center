# Ticket Lifecycle

## Purpose

This document describes the complete lifecycle of a customer support ticket from creation through resolution and closure.

---

# Workflow Overview

Every customer request follows a structured lifecycle to ensure consistency, accountability, and timely resolution.

```
New Ticket
      │
      ▼
Categorisation
      │
      ▼
Priority Assignment
      │
      ▼
Agent Assignment
      │
      ▼
Investigation
      │
      ▼
Customer Communication
      │
      ▼
Resolution
      │
      ▼
Customer Confirmation
      │
      ▼
Ticket Closure
```

---

# Stage 1 — Ticket Creation

Customer requests may originate from:

- Email
- Contact Form
- Live Chat
- Phone
- Social Media

---

# Stage 2 — Categorisation

Each ticket is assigned a category such as:

- General Enquiry
- Sales
- Billing
- Technical Support
- Complaint
- Feedback

---

# Stage 3 — Priority Assignment

The ticket receives a priority level based on urgency and business impact.

- Low
- Medium
- High
- Urgent

---

# Stage 4 — Agent Assignment

Tickets are automatically or manually assigned to the appropriate support agent or team.

---

# Stage 5 — Investigation

The assigned agent:

- Reviews the issue
- Collects required information
- Communicates with the customer
- Documents progress

---

# Stage 6 — Resolution

The issue is resolved using standard support procedures.

If required, the ticket is escalated according to the Escalation Matrix.

---

# Stage 7 — Customer Confirmation

The customer confirms that the issue has been resolved satisfactorily.

---

# Stage 8 — Ticket Closure

The ticket is closed after:

- Resolution is confirmed
- Documentation is complete
- Customer communication is finished

---

# Key Performance Indicators

- First Response Time
- Resolution Time
- SLA Compliance
- Customer Satisfaction
- Reopened Tickets
