# Customer Support Process

## Purpose

This document describes the standard process followed for every customer interaction within the Freshdesk Support Center.

---

# Support Workflow

1. Customer submits a request.
2. Ticket is automatically created.
3. Ticket is categorised.
4. Priority is assigned.
5. Ticket is routed to the appropriate team.
6. Agent investigates the issue.
7. Customer receives updates.
8. Resolution is provided.
9. Customer confirms satisfaction.
10. Ticket is closed.

---

# Customer Communication

Agents should:

- Acknowledge every request promptly.
- Communicate clearly and professionally.
- Provide regular updates.
- Confirm resolution before closing a ticket.

---

# Success Measures

- Faster response times
- Reduced resolution times
- Improved customer satisfaction
- SLA compliance
- Consistent customer experience
