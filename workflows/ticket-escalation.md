# Ticket Escalation Workflow

## Purpose

This document defines the process for escalating customer support tickets that cannot be resolved at the initial support level.

---

# Escalation Objectives

The escalation process ensures that:

- High-priority issues receive timely attention.
- Tickets are assigned to the appropriate level of expertise.
- SLA commitments are maintained.
- Customers receive regular updates throughout the resolution process.

---

# Escalation Levels

## Level 1 – Customer Support Agent

Responsible for:

- General enquiries
- Basic troubleshooting
- Order status
- Account updates

---

## Level 2 – Senior Support Agent

Responsible for:

- Complex customer issues
- Technical investigations
- Reopened tickets
- Process exceptions

---

## Level 3 – Support Manager

Responsible for:

- Customer complaints
- SLA breaches
- Service recovery
- Team coordination

---

## Level 4 – Business Owner / Executive

Responsible for:

- Critical business issues
- VIP customer concerns
- High-impact operational incidents

---

# Escalation Triggers

A ticket should be escalated when:

- SLA response time is at risk.
- The issue cannot be resolved at the current support level.
- Multiple customer follow-ups have been received.
- The issue affects multiple customers.
- Executive approval is required.

---

# Expected Outcomes

- Faster issue resolution
- Clear ownership
- Improved accountability
- Higher customer satisfaction
