# Customer Feedback Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow defines how customer feedback is collected, reviewed, and used to improve service quality.

---

# Feedback Process

1. Ticket resolved.
2. Satisfaction survey sent.
3. Customer submits feedback.
4. Responses analysed.
5. Improvement opportunities identified.
6. Action plans implemented.
7. Performance reviewed.

---

# Metrics

- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Response Rate
- Positive Feedback
- Negative Feedback
- Improvement Trends

---

# Objectives

- Measure customer satisfaction.
- Improve service quality.
- Identify training opportunities.
- Support continuous improvement.
