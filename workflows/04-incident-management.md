# Incident Management Workflow

## Purpose

This workflow defines how service interruptions, system failures, and other incidents are identified, managed, and resolved.

---

# Incident Lifecycle

1. Incident reported
2. Incident logged
3. Severity assessed
4. Incident assigned
5. Investigation begins
6. Resolution implemented
7. Customer notified
8. Incident closed
9. Post-incident review completed

---

# Severity Levels

### Low

Minor issue with limited customer impact.

### Medium

Noticeable service disruption affecting multiple users.

### High

Major operational issue requiring urgent attention.

### Critical

Business-critical outage requiring immediate escalation.

---

# Objectives

- Restore service quickly.
- Minimise customer impact.
- Maintain communication.
- Prevent recurrence.
